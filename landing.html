About Zairza

Zairza Technical Society, founded in 2005 and officially recognized in 2007, is a student-run club at OUTR (formerly CET). We empower students to test theories, innovate, and conquer challenges through our three wings: Robotics, Software, and Design. With 5,001-10,000 members and 174 LinkedIn-associated members, we drive technological advancement through research and mentorship.
